# Tangier TAXI

A petit-taxi simulator for My PC on the real streets of Tangier. Passengers wave from the pavement: stop, press A,
drive them along the green route and get paid by the meter. Crashes cost repairs (and the passenger), traffic rules
cost fines, and the diesel runs out.

- 434 km of Tangier streets from OpenStreetMap, 83 traffic lights, 8 fuel stations, city traffic.
- Fares, tips, late and bump penalties; red light, wrong way, pavement and speeding fines; accident cards with costs.
- Fuel: refuel at a station, or pay for roadside help when you run dry.
- Gearbox: automatic, manual, or manual with clutch (stall included). Speed limiter.
- English, French, Spanish, Arabic. TV remote, game controllers, phone pad.

Install address for the My PC App Store Manager: `https://imad-os.github.io/g_taxi/`
